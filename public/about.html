About us

Made of Memories started in Singapore with one simple idea: no moment from your wedding should be lost.

On your big day, your guests take hundreds of photos and videos, and most of them never leave their phones. We give you one beautiful album they can all add to.

Guests scan a QR code at their table and share their photos, videos and wishes, straight from their own phone. No app, no group chats, nothing to set up.

Afterwards, every moment is in one place. Download the whole album and it’s yours to keep, long after the online album closes.

And it isn’t only weddings. We love collecting memories for any special occasion: engagements, birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, graduations, retreats and family gatherings.
