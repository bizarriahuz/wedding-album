Good to know

Questions couples ask

Do our guests need to download an app?

No. Guests scan the QR code with their phone camera and the album opens in their browser, on iPhone and Android alike.

What if the signal at our venue is weak?

Photos and videos wait on the guest’s phone and send automatically as soon as there’s a connection. If your venue has guest Wi-Fi, we can add the details to your table cards.

Who can see the photos?

You choose. Guests can see everyone’s photos, or only their own while the two of you see them all. Wishes and voice messages are always for the couple only.

How do we get our photos afterwards?

From your private couple’s link, you can download everything in one go: photos, videos, written wishes and voice messages, any time while your album is online.

How long do our photos stay online?

For the hosting time in your package: 1 month with Essential, 3 months with Signature and 6 months with Heirloom, counted from your event. We’ll remind you before it ends. When hosting ends, the album closes and its photos, videos and wishes are permanently deleted from our storage, so please download everything before then.

Can we keep the album online for longer?

Yes. Let us know before your hosting ends and we’ll extend it for you, with a quote for the extra months.

Do we keep our photos after the album closes?

Yes, as long as you’ve downloaded them. The download gives you every photo, video and wish to keep on your own devices, for good. The online album itself is only there for your hosting period.

Who can open our album? Do guests need an account?

No accounts, and no app. Guests open the album by scanning your QR code, which carries a private link, and just type their name. Anyone with that link can add photos, so share it only at your event. You get a separate private couple’s link to manage everything. In private gallery mode, guests see only their own uploads while you see them all.

What can guests upload?

Photos (including iPhone HEIC photos) and videos from the camera or gallery. Each video can be up to about 95 MB, which is roughly a minute of phone video at standard quality. Voice wishes can be up to a minute long.

What if an upload fails, or we need help on the day?

Uploads wait safely on the guest’s phone and send automatically once there’s a connection, so nothing is lost if the signal drops. With Heirloom, we’re on hand at your event for technical support. With every package you can email us, and we’ll help as quickly as we can.

Can the album match our wedding’s look?

Yes. Every album has an elegant ivory and lace look, and with Heirloom we style it to match your invitation’s colours and details.

Is it only for weddings?

Not at all. We love collecting memories for any special occasion: engagements, birthdays, anniversaries, baby showers, graduations, retreats and family gatherings. Tell us about yours.

How far ahead should we book?

Ideally four weeks before your wedding, so there’s time to print your QR cards. Get in touch even if it’s sooner and we’ll do our best.

Still wondering?

Tell us about your wedding and we’ll answer anything.
